# English Learning Topics - Ordered Curriculum

## Level: Beginner (רמת מתחילים)

---

## Topic 1: Present Simple Tense (זמן הווה פשוט)

### 1.1 Introduction to Present Simple
- What is Present Simple and when do we use it
- Habits and routines (הרגלים ושגרה)
- Facts and general truths (עובדות ואמיתות כלליות)
- Time expressions: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day/week/month

### 1.2 Affirmative Sentences (משפטים חיוביים)
- Structure: Subject + Verb (+ Object)
- First person: I/We + base verb
  - Example: I play football. We eat breakfast.
- Second person: You + base verb
  - Example: You study English.
- Third person: He/She/It + verb + s/es
  - Example: He plays football. She watches TV. It works well.
- Rules for adding -s/-es/-ies
  - Regular: play → plays, eat → eats
  - -es ending: watch → watches, go → goes, do → does
  - -ies ending: study → studies, fly → flies

### 1.3 Negative Sentences (משפטים שליליים)
- Structure: Subject + do/does + not + base verb
- I/You/We/They + don't + base verb
  - Example: I don't like coffee. They don't play tennis.
- He/She/It + doesn't + base verb
  - Example: He doesn't watch TV. She doesn't eat meat.
- Important: The main verb returns to base form (doesn't playS ❌ → doesn't play ✅)

### 1.4 Yes/No Questions (שאלות כן/לא)
- Structure: Do/Does + subject + base verb?
- Do + I/you/we/they + base verb?
  - Example: Do you like pizza? Do they play football?
- Does + he/she/it + base verb?
  - Example: Does he work here? Does she speak English?
- Short answers:
  - Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
  - Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.

### 1.5 Wh- Questions (שאלות מידע)
- Structure: Wh- word + do/does + subject + base verb?
- What, Where, When, Who, Why, How
- Examples:
  - What do you do? (מה אתה עושה? - מקצוע)
  - Where does she live? (איפה היא גרה?)
  - When do they arrive? (מתי הם מגיעים?)
  - Why do you study English? (למה אתה לומד אנגלית?)
  - How does it work? (איך זה עובד?)

### 1.6 Frequency Adverbs (תארי תדירות)
- Position in sentence: Subject + adverb + verb
- always (100%) - תמיד
- usually (80%) - בדרך כלל
- often (60%) - לעתים קרובות
- sometimes (40%) - לפעמים
- rarely (20%) - לעתים רחוקות
- never (0%) - אף פעם לא
- Examples:
  - I always wake up at 7 AM.
  - She usually eats lunch at school.
  - They never watch horror movies.

## Topic 2: Personal Pronouns & Possessives (כינויי גוף ושייכות)

### 2.1 Subject Pronouns (כינויי גוף - נושא)
- I (אני - יחיד)
- You (אתה/את - יחיד, אתם/אתן - רבים)
- He (הוא)
- She (היא)
- It (זה/זאת - לדברים וחיות)
- We (אנחנו)
- They (הם/הן)
- When to use each pronoun
- Replacing nouns with pronouns

### 2.2 Object Pronouns (כינויי גוף - מושא)
- me (אותי)
- you (אותך/אותכם)
- him (אותו)
- her (אותה)
- it (את זה)
- us (אותנו)
- them (אותם/אותן)
- Position in sentence: after the verb
- Examples:
  - She likes me. (היא אוהבת אותי)
  - I see him every day. (אני רואה אותו כל יום)
  - They help us. (הם עוזרים לנו)

### 2.3 Possessive Adjectives (תארי שייכות)
- my (שלי)
- your (שלך/שלכם)
- his (שלו)
- her (שלה)
- its (שלו/שלה - לדברים)
- our (שלנו)
- their (שלהם/שלהן)
- Always come before a noun
- Examples:
  - my book (הספר שלי)
  - your house (הבית שלך)
  - their car (המכונית שלהם)

### 2.4 Possessive Pronouns (כינויי שייכות)
- mine (שלי)
- yours (שלך/שלכם)
- his (שלו)
- hers (שלה)
- its (שלו/שלה - לדברים)
- ours (שלנו)
- theirs (שלהם/שלהן)
- Stand alone (replace noun)
- Examples:
  - This book is mine. (הספר הזה שלי)
  - That car is yours. (המכונית ההיא שלך)
  - The house is theirs. (הבית שלהם)

### 2.5 Possessive 's (apostrophe s)
- Showing ownership: noun + 's + noun
- Examples:
  - Tom's book (הספר של טום)
  - My mother's car (המכונית של אמא שלי)
  - The teacher's desk (השולחן של המורה)
- Special cases:
  - Names ending in 's': James's or James'
  - Plural nouns: the students' books (הספרים של התלמידים)

## Topic 3: Articles (מאמרים - a, an, the)

### 3.1 Indefinite Articles - A/An (מאמר לא מוגדר)
- When to use A vs AN
- A + consonant sound
  - a book, a car, a university (u sounds like "yu")
- AN + vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u)
  - an apple, an egg, an hour (h is silent)
- Usage:
  - First mention of something (פעם ראשונה)
    - I see a dog. (אני רואה כלב - כלב כלשהו)
  - Talking about one of many
    - She is a teacher. (היא מורה - אחת מני רבות)
  - Meaning "one"
    - I need a pen. (אני צריך עט אחד)

### 3.2 Definite Article - The (מאמר מוגדר)
- One word for all nouns (the = ה-)
- Usage:
  - Specific/known item (דבר מסוים/ידוע)
    - The dog is big. (הכלב הזה גדול - כלב ספציפי)
  - Second mention
    - I see a dog. The dog is brown. (ראיתי כלב. הכלב חום)
  - Unique things
    - the sun, the moon, the earth, the internet
  - Superlatives
    - the best, the biggest, the most beautiful
  - Specific places
    - the park, the school, the supermarket (when specific)

### 3.3 Zero Article (ללא מאמר)
- When NOT to use articles:
- Plural nouns (general)
  - Dogs are animals. (כלבים הם חיות - בכלל)
  - I like apples. (אני אוהב תפוחים - בכלל)
- Uncountable nouns (general)
  - Water is important. (מים חשובים)
  - I need help. (אני צריך עזרה)
- Names of people
  - Tom, Sarah (not "the Tom")
- Most countries
  - Israel, England, France
  - Exceptions: the USA, the UK
- Meals (general)
  - I eat breakfast at 7. (לא "the breakfast")
- Sports and subjects
  - I play basketball. I study math.

### 3.4 Common Mistakes & Practice
- ❌ I am teacher. → ✅ I am a teacher.
- ❌ I like the dogs. (general) → ✅ I like dogs.
- ❌ A apple is red. → ✅ An apple is red.
- ❌ He is a best student. → ✅ He is the best student.
- Comparing Hebrew to English usage
  - Hebrew often doesn't use articles where English does
  - Practice identifying when each article is needed

## Topic 4: Nouns - Singular & Plural (שמות עצם - יחיד ורבים)

### 4.1 Regular Plurals (רבים רגילים)
- Basic rule: Add -S to most nouns
  - book → books (ספרים)
  - car → cars (מכוניות)
  - dog → dogs (כלבים)
  - pen → pens (עטים)

- Add -ES after: s, ss, sh, ch, x, z
  - bus → buses (אוטובוסים)
  - class → classes (כיתות)
  - dish → dishes (צלחות)
  - watch → watches (שעונים)
  - box → boxes (קופסאות)

- Words ending in consonant + Y: Change Y to -IES
  - baby → babies (תינוקות)
  - city → cities (ערים)
  - story → stories (סיפורים)
  - lady → ladies (גברות)

- Words ending in vowel + Y: Add -S
  - boy → boys (בנים)
  - day → days (ימים)
  - key → keys (מפתחות)
  - toy → toys (צעצועים)

- Words ending in F or FE: Change to -VES
  - knife → knives (סכינים)
  - leaf → leaves (עלים)
  - wife → wives (נשים נשואות)
  - shelf → shelves (מדפים)
  - Exceptions: roof → roofs, chef → chefs

- Words ending in consonant + O: usually add -ES
  - tomato → tomatoes (עגבניות)
  - potato → potatoes (תפוחי אדמה)
  - hero → heroes (גיבורים)
  - Exceptions: photo → photos, piano → pianos

### 4.2 Irregular Plurals (רבים חריגים)
- Common irregular forms (must memorize):
  - man → men (גברים)
  - woman → women (נשים)
  - child → children (ילדים)
  - person → people (אנשים)
  - tooth → teeth (שיניים)
  - foot → feet (רגליים)
  - mouse → mice (עכברים)
  - goose → geese (אווזים)

- No change (same form for singular and plural):
  - sheep → sheep (כבשים)
  - fish → fish (דגים) *can also be fishes
  - deer → deer (צבאים)
  - series → series (סדרות)

### 4.3 Countable Nouns (שמות עצם בני ספירה)
- Can be counted: one, two, three...
- Have singular and plural forms
- Use a/an with singular
- Examples:
  - one apple, two apples (תפוח, תפוחים)
  - a book, three books (ספר, ספרים)
  - one student, ten students (תלמיד, תלמידים)
  - a chair, many chairs (כיסא, כיסאות)

- With numbers and quantifiers:
  - I have two dogs. (יש לי שני כלבים)
  - She reads many books. (היא קוראת הרבה ספרים)
  - There are a few cars. (יש כמה מכוניות)
  - How many apples do you want? (כמה תפוחים אתה רוצה?)

### 4.4 Uncountable Nouns (שמות עצם שאינם בני ספירה)
- Cannot be counted directly
- No plural form
- No a/an (cannot say "a water")
- Always use singular verb

- Common categories:
  - Liquids: water (מים), milk (חלב), coffee (קפה), juice (מיץ)
  - Food: bread (לחם), rice (אורז), meat (בשר), cheese (גבינה)
  - Materials: wood (עץ), paper (נייר), glass (זכוכית), gold (זהב)
  - Abstract: love (אהבה), happiness (אושר), information (מידע), advice (עצה)
  - Activities: homework (שיעורי בית), work (עבודה), traffic (תנועה)
  - Weather: rain (גשם), snow (שלג), weather (מזג אוויר)

- How to count uncountable nouns:
  - Use containers or portions:
    - a glass of water (כוס מים)
    - two cups of coffee (שתי כוסות קפה)
    - three slices of bread (שלוש פרוסות לחם)
    - a piece of advice (עצה אחת)
    - a bottle of milk (בקבוק חלב)

### 4.5 Some/Any with Countable and Uncountable
- SOME (כמה, קצת):
  - Affirmative sentences
  - With countable plural: some books (כמה ספרים)
  - With uncountable: some water (קצת מים)
  - Examples:
    - I have some friends. (יש לי כמה חברים)
    - There is some milk in the fridge. (יש קצת חלב במקרר)
    - She wants some coffee. (היא רוצה קצת קפה)

- ANY (כלשהו, קצת):
  - Questions and negatives
  - With countable plural: any books (ספרים כלשהם)
  - With uncountable: any water (מים כלשהם)
  - Examples:
    - Do you have any money? (יש לך כסף?)
    - I don't have any pets. (אין לי חיות מחמד)
    - Is there any coffee? (יש קפה?)
    - There aren't any chairs. (אין כיסאות)

### 4.6 Common Mistakes (טעויות נפוצות)
- ❌ I have two childs. → ✅ I have two children.
- ❌ She has beautiful tooths. → ✅ She has beautiful teeth.
- ❌ I need an informations. → ✅ I need some information. (uncountable)
- ❌ There are five womans. → ✅ There are five women.
- ❌ I drink two waters. → ✅ I drink two glasses of water.
- ❌ He gave me many advices. → ✅ He gave me much advice / some advice.
- ❌ I like musics. → ✅ I like music. (uncountable)
- ❌ We have much books. → ✅ We have many books. (many + countable)
- ❌ Do you have some money? → ✅ Do you have any money? (questions)
- ❌ I don't have some time. → ✅ I don't have any time. (negative)

## Topic 5: Verb "To Be" - Present (פועל להיות - הווה)

### 5.1 Forms: am, is, are (הצורות הבסיסיות)
- I am (אני)
- You are (אתה/את/אתם/אתן)
- He is (הוא)
- She is (היא)
- It is (זה/זאת)
- We are (אנחנו)
- They are (הם/הן)

### 5.2 Affirmative Sentences (משפטים חיוביים)
- Structure: Subject + am/is/are + complement
- Examples:
  - I am a student. (אני תלמיד)
  - You are smart. (אתה חכם)
  - He is tall. (הוא גבוה)
  - She is a teacher. (היא מורה)
  - It is cold. (קר)
  - We are friends. (אנחנו חברים)
  - They are at home. (הם בבית)

- Uses of "To Be":
  - Identity: I am Tom. She is a doctor.
  - Description: He is tall. They are happy.
  - Location: We are at school. It is on the table.
  - Age: I am 10 years old. (אני בן 10)
  - Nationality: She is American. (היא אמריקאית)
  - Feelings: I am tired. (אני עייף)

### 5.3 Contractions (קיצורים)
- Common in spoken and informal English:
  - I am → I'm
  - You are → You're
  - He is → He's
  - She is → She's
  - It is → It's
  - We are → We're
  - They are → They're

- Examples:
  - I'm happy. (אני שמח)
  - You're late. (אתה מאחר)
  - He's my brother. (הוא אחי)
  - She's beautiful. (היא יפה)
  - It's raining. (יורד גשם)
  - We're ready. (אנחנו מוכנים)
  - They're students. (הם תלמידים)

### 5.4 Negative Sentences (משפטים שליליים)
- Structure: Subject + am/is/are + NOT + complement
- Full forms:
  - I am not (אני לא)
  - You are not (אתה לא)
  - He is not (הוא לא)
  - She is not (היא לא)
  - It is not (זה לא)
  - We are not (אנחנו לא)
  - They are not (הם לא)

- Negative contractions:
  - I'm not (only one form)
  - You aren't / You're not
  - He isn't / He's not
  - She isn't / She's not
  - It isn't / It's not
  - We aren't / We're not
  - They aren't / They're not

- Examples:
  - I'm not tired. (אני לא עייף)
  - He isn't at home. (הוא לא בבית)
  - We aren't ready. (אנחנו לא מוכנים)
  - They're not students. (הם לא תלמידים)

### 5.5 Yes/No Questions (שאלות כן/לא)
- Structure: Am/Is/Are + subject + complement?
- Examples:
  - Am I late? (אני מאחר?)
  - Are you ready? (אתה מוכן?)
  - Is he your brother? (הוא אחיך?)
  - Is she a teacher? (היא מורה?)
  - Is it cold? (קר?)
  - Are we friends? (אנחנו חברים?)
  - Are they at home? (הם בבית?)

- Short answers:
  - Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
  - Yes, you are. / No, you aren't.
  - Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
  - Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
  - Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
  - Yes, we are. / No, we aren't.
  - Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

### 5.6 Wh- Questions (שאלות מידע)
- Structure: Wh- word + am/is/are + subject?
- Who is he? (מי הוא?)
  - He is my friend. (הוא החבר שלי)
- What is your name? (מה שמך?)
  - My name is Tom. (שמי טום)
- Where are you from? (מאיפה אתה?)
  - I'm from Israel. (אני מישראל)
- When is the party? (מתי המסיבה?)
  - It's on Friday. (ביום שישי)
- Why are you sad? (למה אתה עצוב?)
  - I'm sad because... (אני עצוב כי...)
- How old are you? (בן כמה אתה?)
  - I'm 10 years old. (אני בן 10)
- How are you? (מה שלומך?)
  - I'm fine, thank you. (אני בסדר, תודה)

### 5.7 Common Mistakes (טעויות נפוצות)
- ❌ I is a student. → ✅ I am a student.
- ❌ He am tall. → ✅ He is tall.
- ❌ They is at home. → ✅ They are at home.
- ❌ You is my friend. → ✅ You are my friend.
- ❌ I not tired. → ✅ I'm not tired. (need am/is/are)
- ❌ He not happy. → ✅ He isn't happy.
- ❌ Is you ready? → ✅ Are you ready?
- ❌ Where you are? → ✅ Where are you?
- ❌ She is have a car. → ✅ She has a car. (not "is have")
- ❌ I'm have 10 years. → ✅ I'm 10 years old. (age with "be", not "have")

## Topic 6: There is / There are (יש)

### 6.1 Affirmative Sentences (משפטים חיוביים)
- THERE IS + singular noun / uncountable noun
  - There is a book. (יש ספר)
  - There is a dog in the garden. (יש כלב בגינה)
  - There is water in the bottle. (יש מים בבקבוק)
  - There is one teacher. (יש מורה אחד)

- THERE ARE + plural noun
  - There are books. (יש ספרים)
  - There are three dogs. (יש שלושה כלבים)
  - There are many students. (יש הרבה תלמידים)
  - There are flowers in the garden. (יש פרחים בגינה)

### 6.2 Negative Sentences (משפטים שליליים)
- THERE ISN'T (is not) + singular / uncountable
  - There isn't a book. (אין ספר)
  - There isn't any water. (אין מים)
  - There isn't a problem. (אין בעיה)
  - There isn't time. (אין זמן)

- THERE AREN'T (are not) + plural
  - There aren't any books. (אין ספרים)
  - There aren't three dogs. (אין שלושה כלבים)
  - There aren't many people. (אין הרבה אנשים)
  - There aren't any chairs. (אין כיסאות)

### 6.3 Questions (שאלות)
- IS THERE + singular / uncountable?
  - Is there a book? (יש ספר?)
  - Is there water? (יש מים?)
  - Is there a problem? (יש בעיה?)
  - Is there any coffee? (יש קפה?)

- ARE THERE + plural?
  - Are there books? (יש ספרים?)
  - Are there any dogs? (יש כלבים?)
  - Are there many people? (יש הרבה אנשים?)
  - Are there chairs? (יש כיסאות?)

### 6.4 Short Answers (תשובות קצרות)
- Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
  - Is there a book? Yes, there is. (יש ספר? כן, יש)
  - Is there water? No, there isn't. (יש מים? לא, אין)

- Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.
  - Are there books? Yes, there are. (יש ספרים? כן, יש)
  - Are there dogs? No, there aren't. (יש כלבים? לא, אין)

### 6.5 Some/Any Usage (שימוש ב-some/any)
- SOME in affirmative:
  - There is some water. (יש קצת מים)
  - There are some books. (יש כמה ספרים)
  - There is some food. (יש קצת אוכל)

- ANY in questions and negatives:
  - Is there any water? (יש מים?)
  - There isn't any water. (אין מים)
  - Are there any books? (יש ספרים?)
  - There aren't any books. (אין ספרים)

### 6.6 Describing Rooms and Places (תיאור חדרים ומקומות)
- In my room there is... (בחדר שלי יש...)
  - There is a bed. (יש מיטה)
  - There is a desk. (יש שולחן)
  - There are two windows. (יש שני חלונות)
  - There are many books. (יש הרבה ספרים)

- In the classroom there is... (בכיתה יש...)
  - There is a board. (יש לוח)
  - There are thirty chairs. (יש שלושים כיסאות)
  - There is one teacher. (יש מורה אחד)

- In the city there is... (בעיר יש...)
  - There is a park. (יש פארק)
  - There are many shops. (יש הרבה חנויות)
  - There is a museum. (יש מוזיאון)

### 6.7 Common Mistakes (טעויות נפוצות)
- ❌ There are a book. → ✅ There is a book. (singular)
- ❌ There is three dogs. → ✅ There are three dogs. (plural)
- ❌ Is there books? → ✅ Are there books? (plural)
- ❌ There isn't books. → ✅ There aren't books. (plural)
- ❌ There have a problem. → ✅ There is a problem. (not "have")
- ❌ There is many people. → ✅ There are many people. (people = plural)
- ❌ Is there some water? → ✅ Is there any water? (questions = any)
- ❌ There aren't some chairs. → ✅ There aren't any chairs. (negative = any)

## Topic 7: Demonstratives (מילות הצבעה)

### 7.1 This - Singular, Near (זה/זאת - יחיד, קרוב)
- Points to one thing close to you
- This + singular noun
- Examples:
  - This book is mine. (הספר הזה שלי)
  - This is my pen. (זה העט שלי)
  - This apple is red. (התפוח הזה אדום)
  - This is my friend Tom. (זה החבר שלי טום)
  - I like this song. (אני אוהב את השיר הזה)

### 7.2 That - Singular, Far (ההוא/ההיא - יחיד, רחוק)
- Points to one thing far from you
- That + singular noun
- Examples:
  - That book is yours. (הספר ההוא שלך)
  - That is your pen. (זה העט שלך)
  - That car is expensive. (המכונית ההיא יקרה)
  - That is my house. (זה הבית שלי - רחוק)
  - I don't like that movie. (אני לא אוהב את הסרט ההוא)

### 7.3 These - Plural, Near (אלה - רבים, קרוב)
- Points to multiple things close to you
- These + plural noun
- Examples:
  - These books are mine. (הספרים האלה שלי)
  - These are my pens. (אלה העטים שלי)
  - These apples are fresh. (התפוחים האלה טריים)
  - These are my friends. (אלה החברים שלי)
  - I like these shoes. (אני אוהב את הנעליים האלה)

### 7.4 Those - Plural, Far (ההם/ההן - רבים, רחוק)
- Points to multiple things far from you
- Those + plural noun
- Examples:
  - Those books are yours. (הספרים ההם שלך)
  - Those are your pens. (אלה העטים שלך - רחוק)
  - Those cars are expensive. (המכוניות ההן יקרות)
  - Those are my neighbors. (אלה השכנים שלי - רחוק)
  - I don't like those colors. (אני לא אוהב את הצבעים ההם)

### 7.5 As Adjectives vs Pronouns (תארים או כינויים)
- As ADJECTIVES (before a noun):
  - This book (הספר הזה)
  - That car (המכונית ההיא)
  - These apples (התפוחים האלה)
  - Those houses (הבתים ההם)

- As PRONOUNS (standing alone):
  - This is my book. (זה הספר שלי)
  - That is expensive. (זה יקר)
  - These are fresh. (אלה טריים)
  - Those are beautiful. (אלה יפים)

### 7.6 Common Expressions (ביטויים נפוצים)
- This morning (הבוקר)
- This week (השבוע)
- This year (השנה הזאת)
- That day (היום ההוא)
- In those days (בימים ההם)
- These days (בימים אלה - עכשיו)
- That's right! (נכון!)
- That's wrong! (זה לא נכון!)
- This is it! (זהו!)
- What is this? (מה זה?)
- Who is that? (מי זה?)

### 7.7 Common Mistakes (טעויות נפוצות)
- ❌ This books are mine. → ✅ These books are mine. (plural)
- ❌ That apples are red. → ✅ Those apples are red. (plural)
- ❌ These book is good. → ✅ This book is good. (singular)
- ❌ Those dog is big. → ✅ That dog is big. (singular)
- ❌ This are my friends. → ✅ These are my friends. (plural)
- ❌ These is my pen. → ✅ This is my pen. (singular)

## Topic 8: Adjectives (שמות תואר)

### 8.1 What are Adjectives? (מהם שמות תואר?)
- Words that describe nouns
- Answer questions: What kind? Which one? How many?
- Make sentences more interesting and specific
- Examples:
  - big (גדול), small (קטן)
  - beautiful (יפה), ugly (מכוער)
  - happy (שמח), sad (עצוב)
  - new (חדש), old (ישן)
  - fast (מהיר), slow (איטי)

### 8.2 Position in Sentence (מיקום במשפט)
- BEFORE the noun (לפני שם העצם):
  - a big house (בית גדול)
  - a beautiful flower (פרח יפה)
  - an old car (מכונית ישנה)
  - happy children (ילדים שמחים)
  - cold water (מים קרים)

- AFTER "to be" verb (אחרי פועל להיות):
  - The house is big. (הבית גדול)
  - The flower is beautiful. (הפרח יפה)
  - The car is old. (המכונית ישנה)
  - The children are happy. (הילדים שמחים)
  - The water is cold. (המים קרים)

### 8.3 Common Adjectives (שמות תואר נפוצים)
- Size (גודל):
  - big / large (גדול), small / little (קטן)
  - tall (גבוה), short (נמוך)
  - long (ארוך), short (קצר)
  - wide (רחב), narrow (צר)

- Quality (איכות):
  - good (טוב), bad (רע)
  - beautiful (יפה), ugly (מכוער)
  - clean (נקי), dirty (מלוכלך)
  - new (חדש), old (ישן)
  - easy (קל), difficult / hard (קשה)

- Colors (צבעים):
  - red (אדום), blue (כחול), green (ירוק)
  - yellow (צהוב), black (שחור), white (לבן)
  - orange (כתום), purple (סגול), pink (ורוד)
  - brown (חום), gray (אפור)

- Feelings (רגשות):
  - happy (שמח), sad (עצוב)
  - angry (כועס), calm (רגוע)
  - excited (נרגש), bored (משועמם)
  - tired (עייף), energetic (מלא אנרגיה)

- Temperature (טמפרטורה):
  - hot (חם), cold (קר)
  - warm (חמים), cool (צונן)

### 8.4 Opposite Adjectives Pairs (זוגות ניגודים)
- big ↔ small (גדול ↔ קטן)
- tall ↔ short (גבוה ↔ נמוך)
- long ↔ short (ארוך ↔ קצר)
- fat ↔ thin (שמן ↔ רזה)
- heavy ↔ light (כבד ↔ קל)
- fast ↔ slow (מהיר ↔ איטי)
- hot ↔ cold (חם ↔ קר)
- young ↔ old (צעיר ↔ זקן)
- new ↔ old (חדש ↔ ישן)
- clean ↔ dirty (נקי ↔ מלוכלך)
- easy ↔ difficult (קל ↔ קשה)
- good ↔ bad (טוב ↔ רע)
- happy ↔ sad (שמח ↔ עצוב)
- beautiful ↔ ugly (יפה ↔ מכוער)
- cheap ↔ expensive (זול ↔ יקר)
- strong ↔ weak (חזק ↔ חלש)
- full ↔ empty (מלא ↔ ריק)
- right ↔ wrong (נכון ↔ לא נכון)

### 8.5 Adjective Order (סדר שמות התואר)
When using multiple adjectives before a noun:
- Opinion → Size → Age → Color → Origin → Material → Noun
- Examples:
  - a beautiful big old red Italian wooden table
  - a nice small new blue American plastic toy
  - a lovely little young brown dog

- Most common patterns:
  - Opinion + Color: a beautiful blue dress
  - Size + Color: a big red car
  - Age + Color: an old green house
  - Number + Color + Noun: three yellow flowers

### 8.6 -ed vs -ing Adjectives (תארים עם -ed ו -ing)
- -ED adjectives describe HOW YOU FEEL (מה אתה מרגיש):
  - I am bored. (אני משועמם)
  - She is tired. (היא עייפה)
  - He is excited. (הוא נרגש)
  - They are interested. (הם מעוניינים)

- -ING adjectives describe WHAT CAUSES THE FEELING (מה גורם להרגשה):
  - The movie is boring. (הסרט משעמם)
  - The work is tiring. (העבודה מעייפת)
  - The game is exciting. (המשחק מרגש)
  - The book is interesting. (הספר מעניין)

- Common pairs:
  - bored / boring (משועמם / משעמם)
  - tired / tiring (עייף / מעייף)
  - excited / exciting (נרגש / מרגש)
  - interested / interesting (מעוניין / מעניין)
  - surprised / surprising (מופתע / מפתיע)
  - worried / worrying (מודאג / מדאיג)
  - confused / confusing (מבולבל / מבלבל)
  - amazed / amazing (נדהם / מדהים)

### 8.7 Common Mistakes (טעויות נפוצות)
- ❌ He is a boy tall. → ✅ He is a tall boy. (adjective before noun)
- ❌ This is car red. → ✅ This is a red car.
- ❌ I have a book interested. → ✅ I have an interesting book.
- ❌ The movie is bored. → ✅ The movie is boring. (movie causes boredom)
- ❌ I am boring. → ✅ I am bored. (you feel bored)
- ❌ She is a beautiful very girl. → ✅ She is a very beautiful girl.
- ❌ They are childs happy. → ✅ They are happy children.

## Topic 9: Prepositions of Place (מילות יחס - מקום)

### 9.1 In, On, At (ב-, על, אצל)
- IN (בתוך):
  - Inside a container or space
  - in the box (בקופסה)
  - in the room (בחדר)
  - in the garden (בגינה)
  - in the car (במכונית)
  - in the bag (בתיק)
  - in the water (במים)
  - in the sky (בשמיים)

- ON (על):
  - On a surface
  - on the table (על השולחן)
  - on the wall (על הקיר)
  - on the floor (על הרצפה)
  - on the chair (על הכיסא)
  - on the shelf (על המדף)
  - on the page (על הדף)

- AT (אצל, ליד, ב-):
  - At a specific point or location
  - at the door (ליד הדלת)
  - at the window (ליד החלון)
  - at the bus stop (בתחנת האוטובוס)
  - at school (בבית הספר)
  - at home (בבית)
  - at work (בעבודה)

### 9.2 Under, Over, Above, Below (מתחת, מעל)
- UNDER (מתחת ל-):
  - Directly below, with contact or close
  - under the table (מתחת לשולחן)
  - under the bed (מתחת למיטה)
  - under the bridge (מתחת לגשר)
  - under the tree (מתחת לעץ)

- OVER (מעל ל-):
  - Directly above, often with movement
  - over the bridge (מעל לגשר)
  - over the table (מעל לשולחן)
  - fly over the city (לטוס מעל העיר)

- ABOVE (מעל ל-):
  - Higher than, not necessarily directly above
  - above the clouds (מעל העננים)
  - above sea level (מעל פני הים)
  - above the door (מעל הדלת)

- BELOW (מתחת ל-):
  - Lower than, not necessarily directly under
  - below the surface (מתחת לפני השטח)
  - below zero (מתחת לאפס)

### 9.3 Next to, Beside, Between, Among (ליד, בין)
- NEXT TO / BESIDE (ליד):
  - At the side of
  - next to the door (ליד הדלת)
  - beside the window (ליד החלון)
  - sit next to me (שב לידי)
  - beside the school (ליד בית הספר)

- BETWEEN (בין):
  - In the middle of two things
  - between the trees (בין העצים - שניים)
  - between you and me (בינך לביני)
  - between the houses (בין הבתים)

- AMONG (בין, בתוך):
  - In the middle of three or more things
  - among the trees (בין העצים - הרבה)
  - among friends (בין חברים)
  - among the crowd (בתוך הקהל)

### 9.4 In front of, Behind (מול, מאחורי)
- IN FRONT OF (מול, לפני):
  - At the front side
  - in front of the house (מול הבית)
  - in front of the class (לפני הכיתה)
  - stand in front of me (תעמוד לפני)

- BEHIND (מאחורי):
  - At the back side
  - behind the door (מאחורי הדלת)
  - behind the tree (מאחורי העץ)
  - sit behind me (שב מאחורי)

### 9.5 Near, Far from (קרוב ל-, רחוק מ-)
- NEAR (קרוב ל-):
  - Close to, not far
  - near the school (קרוב לבית הספר)
  - near the park (קרוב לפארק)
  - I live near here. (אני גר קרוב לכאן)

- FAR FROM (רחוק מ-):
  - Not close, distant
  - far from the city (רחוק מהעיר)
  - far from home (רחוק מהבית)
  - Is it far from here? (זה רחוק מכאן?)

### 9.6 Common Expressions and Idioms (ביטויים נפוצים)
- at the top (בראש, בפסגה)
- at the bottom (בתחתית)
- in the middle (באמצע)
- in the corner (בפינה)
- on the corner (בפינת הרחוב)
- on the right (מימין)
- on the left (משמאל)
- in the center (במרכז)
- inside (בפנים)
- outside (בחוץ)
- upstairs (למעלה, בקומה העליונה)
- downstairs (למטה, בקומה התחתונה)

### 9.7 Common Mistakes (טעויות נפוצות)
- ❌ The book is in the table. → ✅ The book is on the table.
- ❌ I live at Tel Aviv. → ✅ I live in Tel Aviv. (cities = in)
- ❌ He sits in the chair. → ✅ He sits on the chair.
- ❌ The picture is in the wall. → ✅ The picture is on the wall.
- ❌ I am at home. (correct) but ❌ I am at the home. → ✅ I am at home. (no "the")
- ❌ Between three people. → ✅ Among three people. (3+ = among)
- ❌ The cat is under of the bed. → ✅ The cat is under the bed. (no "of")

## Topic 10: Prepositions of Time (מילות יחס - זמן)

### 10.1 At (ב- לשעות ותקופות)
- AT for specific times:
  - Clock times: at 8 o'clock (בשעה 8)
  - at 7:30 (בשעה 7:30)
  - at noon (בצהריים)
  - at midnight (בחצות)
  - at night (בלילה)
  - at sunrise (בזריחה)
  - at sunset (בשקיעה)

- AT for holidays/festivals:
  - at Christmas (בחג המולד)
  - at Easter (בפסחא)
  - at Hanukkah (בחנוכה)

- AT for special expressions:
  - at the moment (ברגע זה)
  - at present (כרגע)
  - at the same time (באותו זמן)
  - at the weekend (בסוף שבוע - British)

### 10.2 On (ב- לימים ותאריכים)
- ON for days:
  - on Monday (ביום שני)
  - on Tuesday (ביום שלישי)
  - on Friday (ביום שישי)
  - on the weekend (בסוף שבוע - American)
  - on weekdays (בימי חול)
  - on my birthday (ביום ההולדת שלי)

- ON for dates:
  - on July 4th (ב-4 ביולי)
  - on January 1st (ב-1 בינואר)
  - on December 25th (ב-25 בדצמבר)

- ON for specific days:
  - on Christmas Day (ביום חג המולד)
  - on New Year's Day (ביום ראש השנה)
  - on that day (באותו יום)

### 10.3 In (ב- לתקופות ארוכות)
- IN for months:
  - in January (בינואר)
  - in July (ביולי)
  - in December (בדצמבר)

- IN for years:
  - in 2024 (ב-2024)
  - in 2010 (ב-2010)
  - in the 1990s (בשנות ה-90)

- IN for seasons:
  - in spring (באביב)
  - in summer (בקיץ)
  - in autumn/fall (בסתיו)
  - in winter (בחורף)

- IN for parts of the day:
  - in the morning (בבוקר)
  - in the afternoon (אחר הצהריים)
  - in the evening (בערב)
  - Exception: at night (בלילה)

- IN for centuries:
  - in the 21st century (במאה ה-21)
  - in the past (בעבר)
  - in the future (בעתיד)

### 10.4 For, Since, During (במשך, מאז, במהלך)
- FOR (במשך):
  - Duration of time
  - for two hours (במשך שעתיים)
  - for three days (במשך שלושה ימים)
  - for a week (במשך שבוע)
  - for a long time (במשך זמן רב)
  - for five years (במשך חמש שנים)

- SINCE (מאז):
  - Starting point in time
  - since Monday (מאז יום שני)
  - since 2020 (מאז 2020)
  - since yesterday (מאז אתמול)
  - since last week (מאז שבוע שעבר)
  - since I was a child (מאז שהייתי ילד)

- DURING (במהלך):
  - Throughout a period
  - during the movie (במהלך הסרט)
  - during class (במהלך השיעור)
  - during the summer (במהלך הקיץ)
  - during my vacation (במהלך החופשה שלי)

### 10.5 Before, After, Until (לפני, אחרי, עד)
- BEFORE (לפני):
  - Earlier than
  - before breakfast (לפני ארוחת הבוקר)
  - before school (לפני בית הספר)
  - before 8 o'clock (לפני שעה 8)
  - before Monday (לפני יום שני)

- AFTER (אחרי):
  - Later than
  - after lunch (אחרי ארוחת הצהריים)
  - after school (אחרי בית הספר)
  - after 5 o'clock (אחרי שעה 5)
  - after the movie (אחרי הסרט)

- UNTIL / TILL (עד):
  - Up to a certain time
  - until 5 o'clock (עד שעה 5)
  - until Monday (עד יום שני)
  - until next week (עד שבוע הבא)
  - I'll wait until you come. (אחכה עד שתבוא)

### 10.6 Common Time Expressions (ביטויי זמן נפוצים)
- in time (בזמן, לא מאוחר)
  - We arrived in time. (הגענו בזמן)

- on time (בדיוק בזמן)
  - The bus is on time. (האוטובוס בזמן)

- from...to (מ...עד)
  - from Monday to Friday (מיום שני עד יום שישי)
  - from 9 to 5 (מ-9 עד 5)

- by (עד, לא יאוחר מ-)
  - Finish by 5 o'clock. (סיים עד שעה 5)
  - by tomorrow (עד מחר)

- ago (לפני - בעבר)
  - two days ago (לפני יומיים)
  - a week ago (לפני שבוע)

- last (ה...שעבר)
  - last week (שבוע שעבר)
  - last year (שנה שעברה)

- next (ה...הבא)
  - next week (שבוע הבא)
  - next month (חודש הבא)

- this (ה...הזה)
  - this week (השבוע)
  - this year (השנה)

### 10.7 Common Mistakes (טעויות נפוצות)
- ❌ in Monday → ✅ on Monday (days = on)
- ❌ at July → ✅ in July (months = in)
- ❌ on 8 o'clock → ✅ at 8 o'clock (times = at)
- ❌ in the night → ✅ at night (exception)
- ❌ in weekend → ✅ at/on the weekend
- ❌ since two hours → ✅ for two hours (duration = for)
- ❌ for Monday → ✅ since Monday (starting point = since)
- ❌ I study English since 3 years. → ✅ I study English for 3 years.
- ❌ on the morning → ✅ in the morning
- ❌ at December 25th → ✅ on December 25th (dates = on)
